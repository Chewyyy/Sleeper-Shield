How the model works

Player value blends league-specific fantasy output, recent form, positional scarcity, age curve, health/status, start rate, and roster format.

Draft pick value starts with round value, applies year discount, then adjusts based on the original roster’s strength so a likely early 1st is worth more than a likely late 1st.

Trade fairness compares raw value and then adjusts for how each side’s positional needs change after the deal.

Limitations: this does not know private trade talks, player news context, contract leagues, or off-platform draft capital. Use it as a decision aid, not a final authority.
